About

Hi, I’m Adam Distrola — a self-taught full-stack web developer with a passion for turning ideas into clean, functional, and visually engaging websites. I specialize in responsive front-end design and SaaS-style full-stack applications, using technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, and MongoDB.

I’ve built and deployed real-world projects including Novelmatic, an AI-powered manuscript formatting app; Apex Auto Detailing, a modern business landing page; and Quicksilver Ledger, a dynamic coin melt value calculator.

With a background that blends entrepreneurship, design, and technical problem-solving, I focus on building web experiences that are as practical as they are beautiful — crafted to help creators, small businesses, and startups grow online.
